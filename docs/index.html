Sand Technologies FDE Application

Two unsolicited technical artifacts, read together.

Sand's own public position is "Foundations Over Features": that generative AI and dashboards built on top of broken or ungrounded data are the wrong first move, and that the unglamorous work — finding out where data can't be trusted, then acting on data once it can be — is the right one. Both artifacts try to demonstrate that kind of work directly, rather than describe it.

Artifact One — Data Quality

Can this data be trusted at all? A deterministic audit of Katsina State's health reporting, ranking all 34 LGAs by how untrustworthy their reported data is — reporting attrition, biologically impossible coverage values — against live-pulled MSDAT and GRID3 data.

View the report →

Artifact Two — HR Reallocation

Given trustworthy data, what action does it justify? Reads Borno State's own published PHC worker-headcount audit and proposes a specific, mathematically optimal set of staff transfers between LGAs — zero-sum, no new hires.
